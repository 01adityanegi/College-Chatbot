About Us

We are the World's Best Polytechnic College

Satyanand Dimri Government Polytechnic, Barkot (Uttarkashi) is a premier technical institution in Uttarakhand. Established with the vision of providing high-quality technical education to the youth of the region, we are committed to excellence in engineering and technology.

Our campus is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, a well-stocked library, and modern classrooms. We focus on holistic development, encouraging students to participate in sports, cultural activities, and technical fests. Affiliated to the Uttarakhand Board of Technical Education Roorkee (UBTER), our curriculum is designed to meet industry standards.
